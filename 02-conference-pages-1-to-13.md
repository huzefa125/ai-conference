# Conference-Specific Content: Conferences 1–13

Each conference block covers: suggested name, SEO title and meta description, home-page hero, About the Conference, Why Attend, Who Should Attend, and Scientific Sessions (tracks). Use these with the shared pages in `01-common-pages.md`.

Replace `[BRAND]`, `[YEAR]`, `[DATES]`, and `[CITY]`. Names are deliberately generic. Check your final names against existing events so you don't overlap with another organizer's trademark.

## 1. Global Summit on Nursing Science & Practice

**SEO title:** Nursing Science Conference [YEAR] | [CITY] & Virtual | [BRAND]
**Meta description:** Join nurses, educators, and researchers at the Global Summit on Nursing Science & Practice [YEAR] in [CITY]. Present research, earn certificates, and network worldwide.

**Hero:** *Evidence at the Bedside: Advancing Nursing Science for Safer, Smarter Care*
[DATES] · [CITY] · Hybrid Event · [Submit Abstract] [Register]

**About the conference**
Nurses are the largest group of healthcare professionals, and their decisions shape patient outcomes every hour of every day. The Global Summit on Nursing Science & Practice brings together clinical nurses, nurse scientists, educators, and leaders to share research that is changing how care is delivered. Over three days, delegates will explore new evidence on patient safety, digital health, workforce wellbeing, and specialty practice through keynotes, oral and poster sessions, and hands-on workshops. Whether you lead a unit, teach in a classroom, or are just starting your research career, you will leave with ideas you can apply the following Monday.

**Why attend**
- Present your research to an international nursing audience and get published in the abstract book
- Learn practical, evidence-based interventions from frontline experts
- Build collaborations with nursing faculties and hospitals across [XX]+ countries
- Get a certificate of participation that documents your contact hours
- Join the Young Nurse Researcher Forum and compete for the Best Poster award

**Who should attend**
Registered nurses, nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists, nurse educators and deans, nurse managers and CNOs, nursing students, midwives, public health nurses, healthcare quality officers.

**Scientific sessions**
1. **Evidence-Based Nursing Practice**: Turning research into protocols, guidelines, and bedside decisions.
2. **Patient Safety & Quality Improvement**: Falls, pressure injuries, medication errors, and the culture of safety.
3. **Critical Care & Emergency Nursing**: ICU care, triage, resuscitation, and rapid response systems.
4. **Nursing Informatics & Digital Health**: EHR optimization, telehealth, AI decision support, and wearables.
5. **Nursing Leadership & Management**: Staffing models, magnet culture, and transformational leadership.
6. **Oncology & Palliative Nursing**: Symptom management, survivorship, and end-of-life care.
7. **Pediatric & Neonatal Nursing**: Family-centered care from NICU to adolescence.
8. **Mental Health & Psychiatric Nursing**: Therapeutic communication, de-escalation, and recovery models.
9. **Gerontological Nursing**: Frailty, dementia care, and aging in place.
10. **Community & Public Health Nursing**: Home care, school nursing, and population health.
11. **Nurse Workforce Wellbeing**: Burnout, resilience, retention, and safe staffing.
12. **Advanced Practice Nursing**: Scope of practice, prescribing, and APRN-led clinics.
13. **Infection Prevention & Control**: Hand hygiene, AMR stewardship, and outbreak response.
14. **Wound Care & Tissue Viability**: Chronic wounds, dressings, and new therapies.
