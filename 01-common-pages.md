## 2. Call for Abstracts / Submit Abstract

### Why submit?
Presenting at [CONFERENCE NAME] puts your work in front of an international audience of peers, collaborators, and potential employers. Accepted abstracts are published in the official Conference Abstract Book [with a DOI, if applicable] and are eligible for Best Presentation awards.

### Who can submit?
Researchers, physicians, surgeons, nurses, allied health professionals, academics, PhD and master's students, industry scientists, and policymakers working in a field related to the conference theme.

### Presentation categories
| Category | Format | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Keynote / Plenary | By invitation of the Scientific Committee | 30–40 min |
| Oral Presentation | In-person or virtual | 20 min + 5 min Q&A |
| Poster Presentation | Printed poster (in-person) or e-poster (virtual) | 10–15 min incl. discussion |
| Workshop / Symposium | Proposal of 60–90 min, multiple speakers | 60–90 min |
| Young Researcher Forum | Students and early-career researchers (≤5 years post-PhD) | 15 min + 5 min Q&A |
| E-Poster | Virtual display only | Asynchronous |

### Abstract guidelines
1. **Language:** English only.
2. **Length:** 250–300 words (excluding title, authors, and affiliations).
3. **Structure:** Background / Objective · Methods · Results · Conclusion. Case reports and reviews may use free-form structure.
4. **Title:** Maximum 20 words, sentence case, no abbreviations.
5. **Authors:** Full names, affiliations, and country. Underline the presenting author.
6. **Biography:** 100–150 words for the presenting author, with a professional photograph (JPEG, minimum 600×600 px).
7. **Keywords:** 3–5 keywords.
8. **Format:** Download the [Abstract Template] (Word). PDF submissions are not accepted.
9. **Originality:** Abstracts must be original and not previously published in a peer-reviewed journal.
10. **Ethics:** Studies involving humans or animals must have ethics committee approval.

### Review process
- An acknowledgement email is sent within 24 hours of submission.
- Each abstract is reviewed by at least two Scientific Committee members.
- A decision is sent within **5–7 working days**.
- Accepted presenters must complete registration by the deadline on the acceptance letter to keep their slot in the program.

## 9. Speaker & Presentation Guidelines

**Oral presenters**
- Prepare slides in 16:9 PowerPoint format. Send them to [EMAIL] at least 7 days before the event, and bring a backup on a USB drive.
- Keep to your time slot. Session chairs will stop presentations that run over.
- Report to the session room 15 minutes before your session starts.
- Virtual presenters: test your connection, camera, and microphone during the scheduled tech check. Pre-recorded videos (MP4, max 20 min) are accepted as backup.

**Poster presenters**
- Poster size: A0 portrait (841 × 1189 mm).
- Include title, authors, affiliations, introduction, methods, results, conclusion, and references.
- Mount your poster during the first morning break and stand beside it during the poster session.
- E-posters: a single-slide PDF plus an optional 5-minute narrated video.
